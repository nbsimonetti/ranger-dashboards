Methodology & sources

Data source

FDIC Summary of Deposits (SOD) — the annual, branch-level census of deposits at every FDIC-insured branch, reported as of June 30 each year. Pulled live from the FDIC BankFind Suite API (banks.data.fdic.gov/api/sod); free, no key. This dashboard uses every Texas branch and focuses the tables and map on the 33-county footprint.

Definitions

  • Deposits — DEPSUMBR, total deposits assigned to the branch ($000s). Deposits are booked to the branch of record, which can overstate headquarters counties for banks that sweep deposits centrally.
  • Market share — a bank's deposits in a county ÷ all deposits in that county.
  • HHI — Herfindahl-Hirschman Index, the sum of squared percentage market shares in a county (0–10,000). Above 2,500 is considered highly concentrated; below 1,500, competitive.
  • Deposits per branch — TX deposits ÷ TX branches; a rough productivity proxy, not adjusted for branch age or market.

Limitations

  • SOD is annual and dated June 30 — it lags current balances and misses intra-year branch changes.
  • Branch-of-record booking inflates deposits in counties that host a bank's main office or deposit-gathering hub.
  • Credit unions (NCUA-insured) are not in SOD; the competitive picture is bank-only.
