Methodology & sources

Data source

NPPES NPI Registry — the national provider registry (CMS), pulled live from the public API (npiregistry.cms.gov/api); free, no key. Texas dental taxonomies (general dentistry, orthodontics, oral surgery, endodontics, periodontics, pediatric, prosthodontics) plus optometry, bucketed by ZIP prefix to work around the API's 1,200-record-per-query cap and de-duplicated by NPI.

Prospect score (0–100)

A transparent heuristic: base 40, plus practice tenure (sweet spot 5–25 years), solo-proprietor status (the target relationship, +20), contactability (a listed phone), and higher-capex specialties (oral surgery, orthodontics, periodontics). New practices (<3 yrs) and long-tenured ones (>35 yrs) are flagged for startup and succession lending respectively.

Limitations

  • NPPES is an administrative registry — it has no revenue, practice size, or existing-banking-relationship data. Solo-proprietor is self-reported at enrollment.
  • Tenure is inferred from NPI enumeration date, not actual practice-opening date.
  • Dentists bill little Medicare, so (unlike the physician dashboard) there is no Medicare-volume signal; scoring is registry-based.
