Methodology & sources

Data source

SBA 7(a) & 504 FOIA borrower data (the same free public dataset behind the Loan Maturity dashboard) — real originations carrying borrower, NAICS code, county, lender, amount, and rate. Aggregated here by NAICS sector, county, and lender, with agriculture (NAICS 11) broken out.

Coverage note. SBA loans are a subset of small-business lending — conventional (non-SBA) bank small-business and farm loans are not here. The definitive competitive census is the FFIEC CRA small-business/small-farm disclosure data, but ffiec.gov bot-blocks automated pulls (HTTP 403) and requires a manual bulk download; and USDA NASS agricultural context needs a free API key. Both are wired-ready augmentation paths — set them up to layer the full market over this SBA baseline.

Limitations

  • SBA-only — undercounts conventional bank small-business and ag lending.
  • NAICS sector is as-reported on the SBA record; agriculture excludes agriculture-adjacent processing/wholesale.
  • 504 partner-bank names are sometimes redacted in the source.
