CRE Environmental Screening

Environmental records screen for Texas commercial real estate collateral. Pick a CRE parcel, enter coordinates or click the map, and the page checks every listed federal and Texas site within the standard search distance, applies the Phase I triggers in Section 8.3 of the Ranger Bank Credit Policy and SBA's environmental rules, and prints a summary for the credit file.

Historical sources for the Phase I

A records screen does not show past uses that were never permitted. These sources are where an environmental professional looks; they open at the subject location where the site allows it.

Screen many properties

Records lists are checked for every property. Live layers (flood zone, oil and gas wells) add two requests per property and are throttled, so a long list takes a few minutes with them on.
