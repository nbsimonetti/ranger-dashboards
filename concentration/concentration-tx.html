Methodology & sources

Data source

FDIC BankFind Suite API — call-report (Consolidated Reports of Condition & Income) financial data for every FDIC-insured institution, pulled live from banks.data.fdic.gov; free, no key. Institution names, cities and counties come from the /institutions endpoint; the financial line items from /financials, joined by FDIC certificate number (CERT). The dashboard analyzes every active Texas-chartered bank (home state STALP:TX) at the latest available quarter-end.

Concentration measures

The 2006 Interagency Guidance on Concentrations in Commercial Real Estate Lending sets two screening thresholds that trigger heightened risk-management expectations:

  • C&D concentration — construction & land-development loans ≥ 100% of total capital.
  • CRE concentration — total commercial-real-estate loans ≥ 300% of total capital, and CRE grew ≥ 50% over the prior 36 months.

This dashboard applies the two ratio thresholds (100% C&D, 300% CRE). It does not apply the CRE-growth leg of the guidance — that requires a time series and is out of scope for this headline snapshot — so a bank flagged over 300% CRE here meets the level test but is not automatically a supervisory concern.

Definitions (call-report fields used)

  • CRE loans = construction & land development (LNRECONS) + multifamily (LNREMULT) + nonfarm nonresidential (LNRENRES).
  • C&D loans = construction & land development (LNRECONS).
  • Capital = Tier 1 capital (RBCT1J) + allowance for loan & lease losses (LNATRES). This is the standard proxy for total risk-based capital used in the supervisory CRE screen. Where a bank does not report Tier 1 capital, total equity (EQ) + ALLL is substituted; the basis actually used is shown in the freshness stamp.
  • CRE ratio = CRE loans ÷ capital × 100. C&D ratio = C&D loans ÷ capital × 100.
  • CRE / assets = CRE loans ÷ total assets (ASSET). CRE / loans = CRE loans ÷ net loans & leases (LNLSNET).
  • ROA — return on assets (ROA, year-to-date, annualized by the FDIC). Noncurrent loans — noncurrent-to-total-loans % (NPERFV).

Limitations

  • Owner-occupied CRE is included. Call-report headline data does not split owner-occupied from investor nonfarm-nonresidential loans; the strict guidance excludes owner-occupied. This measure therefore overstates investor-CRE concentration for banks with heavy owner-occupied books.
  • Level test only. The 50%-growth leg of the CRE guidance is not applied (see above).
  • Capital is a proxy. Tier 1 + ALLL approximates but does not exactly equal total risk-based capital, especially for banks electing the Community Bank Leverage Ratio framework that do not report risk-based figures.
  • Call reports are quarterly and dated the quarter-end; they lag current balances and reflect a single point in time.
  • This is headline concentration screening, not underwriting — it says nothing about collateral, structure, geography or borrower quality within the CRE book.
