Methodology & sources

Data sources

FDIC BankFind Suite — two free, no-key APIs pulled live: the Institutions endpoint (banks.data.fdic.gov/api/institutions, every active and historical FDIC-insured institution headquartered in Texas) and the Failures endpoint (banks.data.fdic.gov/api/failures, TX failures and assistance transactions). Institutions are filtered on STALP:TX, failures on PSTALP:TX.

Definitions

  • Active charter — ACTIVE = 1. Counts distinct FDIC charters (institutions), not branches, holding companies, or credit unions (NCUA-insured credit unions are not in this data).
  • De-novo formation — a charter's establishment year, from ESTYMD (parsed MM/DD/YYYY).
  • Exit — an inactive charter's end year, from ENDEFYMD (active charters carry the sentinel 12/31/9999). An exit covers any way a charter disappears: acquisition/merger, absorption into an affiliate, voluntary closure, or failure.
  • Failure — an FDIC-resolved failure or open-bank assistance transaction (RESTYPE), a subset of exits. QBFASSET / QBFDEP are assets / deposits at failure; COST is the FDIC's estimated cost ($000s).
  • Active-count series — reconstructed: a charter is counted in year Y when it was established on or before Y and had not yet ended after Y.

Limitations & caveats

  • ENDEFYMD does not distinguish a merger/absorption from a voluntary closure — the exit figures combine both. Genuine failures are isolated separately from the Failures endpoint.
  • This is charter-level structure. Branch openings, closures, and relocations are not shown (see the Deposit Market Share dashboard for the branch network).
  • The active-count series is a reconstruction and can slightly undercount the earliest years if a long-defunct charter has been purged from FDIC's index; it is reliable across the modern consolidation era.
  • Counts are by headquarters county / state (STALP, COUNTY), so a multi-market bank appears only in its home county. Failure counties are recovered by a CERT join to the institutions file and are blank where no match exists.
  • ASSET for an inactive charter is its last reported figure before ceasing, not an inflation-adjusted value.
